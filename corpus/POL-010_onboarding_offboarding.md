# Onboarding and Offboarding Procedures

Document ID: POL-010
Owner: People Team (People Operations), with IT and Information Security
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: All new and departing employees and interns of Lumora Systems, Inc., their managers, and onboarding buddies.

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was created for an educational AI engineering project.

## 1. Purpose

A consistent onboarding experience helps new employees become productive, connected, and compliant quickly, and a consistent offboarding process protects departing employees, their colleagues, and Company data. This procedure sets out the required steps, owners, and deadlines for each phase.

## 2. Roles

- **People Operations:** sends the offer packet, collects employment eligibility documents, enrolls the employee in payroll and benefits, and tracks completion of required training.
- **IT:** ships or prepares equipment (POL-011), creates accounts, and manages access.
- **Hiring manager:** prepares the 30-60-90 day plan, assigns an onboarding buddy, and holds check-ins.
- **Onboarding buddy:** a peer who answers day-to-day questions during the first 90 days.
- **New employee:** completes required forms, training, and acknowledgments by their deadlines.

## 3. Before Day One (Pre-boarding)

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Signed offer letter and background check complete | People Operations | Before start date is confirmed |
| Equipment request submitted (laptop model by role) | Hiring manager | At least 10 business days before start |
| Laptop shipped to remote/hybrid new hires or set up at office | IT | Delivered at least 2 business days before start |
| Accounts created (SSO, email, chat) | IT | 1 business day before start |
| Onboarding buddy assigned | Hiring manager | 5 business days before start |
| Welcome email with first-day agenda | People Operations | 3 business days before start |

## 4. First Day Checklist

On day one, the new employee should:

1. Attend New Hire Orientation (9:30 to 12:00 local time, in person or by video).
2. Activate their SSO account, enroll in multi-factor authentication, and set up the password manager (POL-007 section 3).
3. Confirm the laptop is enrolled in device management and connect to the VPN.
4. Review and acknowledge the Code of Conduct (POL-001) and the Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy (POL-007) in PeopleHub.
5. Meet with their manager to review the role, the 30-60-90 day plan, and team norms.
6. Meet their onboarding buddy.

## 5. First Week Requirements

| Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Employment eligibility verification (US Form I-9 Section 2 completed by People Operations; Canada and UK right-to-work checks) | Within 3 business days of the start date (US I-9 Section 1 must be completed by the employee on or before day one) |
| Payroll setup: direct deposit and tax withholding forms in PeopleHub | Within 5 business days |
| Security Awareness training | Within 7 calendar days |
| Emergency contact information | Within 7 calendar days |
| Review of team on-call and support processes (if applicable) | Within the first week |

## 6. First 30 Days

| Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Benefits enrollment in BenefitsHub (POL-008 section 3) | Within 30 days of start date |
| Respectful Workplace training (POL-001 section 15) | Within 30 days |
| Data Privacy training (Restricted-access roles only) | Within 30 days |
| Conflict of Interest disclosure (POL-001 section 8) | Within 30 days |
| 401(k) election or opt-out (US) | Within 30 days (auto-enrollment at 4% applies otherwise) |
| Home office stipend claim, if eligible (POL-011 section 5) | May be claimed at any time within the first 90 days |
| 30-day check-in with manager | Day 30 |

## 7. 60 and 90 Days

- **60-day check-in:** the manager and employee review progress against the 30-60-90 plan and adjust goals.
- **90-day introductory period review:** the manager completes the introductory period review in PeopleHub. Completion of the introductory period is a requirement for remote work eligibility (POL-004 section 3.2) and negative PTO balances (POL-002 section 8). The introductory period may be extended once by up to 60 days.
- The buddy relationship formally ends at 90 days.

## 8. Onboarding for Interns and Contractors

Interns follow the same first-day and first-week steps, complete Security Awareness and Respectful Workplace training, and have a mentor in place of a buddy. Contractors receive accounts and equipment only as specified in their statement of work, must complete Security Awareness training before receiving access to Confidential data, and do not enroll in Lumora benefits.

## 9. Internal Transfers

Employees moving to a new team complete a transfer checklist: access reviewed and adjusted by IT within 5 business days, new 30-60-90 plan from the new manager, and updated work arrangement if applicable (POL-004 section 4).

## 10. Offboarding

### 10.1 Notice

Employees who resign are asked to give at least 2 weeks' notice (4 weeks for people managers and Director-level and above). Notice should be given in writing to the manager, who notifies People Operations through PeopleHub within 1 business day.

### 10.2 Offboarding checklist

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge transfer plan and handover document | Departing employee and manager | Before last day |
| Exit interview offered | People Operations | During the final 2 weeks |
| Access to all systems revoked | IT | By 6:00 pm local time on the last day (immediately for involuntary terminations) |
| Return of laptop, badge, and all equipment (POL-011 section 9) | Departing employee | Within 10 business days after the last day (prepaid return kit provided for remote employees) |
| Final pay including any required PTO payout (POL-002 section 11) | Payroll | According to state or local law |
| Benefits end and COBRA notice (POL-008 section 15) | Benefits | Coverage ends last day of the month; COBRA notice within 14 days |
| Expense reports submitted | Departing employee | Before last day |

### 10.3 Confidentiality obligations

Departing employees must not retain Lumora Confidential or Restricted information, must delete any Lumora information from personal devices enrolled in app management, and remain bound by their confidentiality agreement after employment ends.

## 11. Using the HR Assistant for Onboarding

The HR assistant can generate a personalized onboarding checklist for a new hire based on their employment type, location, and work arrangement, with links to the relevant policies. The checklist is guidance only; official task tracking happens in PeopleHub.
