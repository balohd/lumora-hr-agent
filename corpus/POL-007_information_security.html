Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy

Document ID: POL-007
Owner: Information Security
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: All employees, interns, and contractors with access to Lumora systems or data.

Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was created for an educational AI engineering project.

1. Purpose

Lumora's customers trust us with their data. This policy sets out the minimum security practices every person with access to Lumora systems must follow to protect Company and customer information, and defines acceptable use of Lumora technology. It applies wherever work is performed, including Lumora offices, homes, and any temporary location approved under POL-004 or POL-005.

2. Data Classification

All Lumora information is classified into one of four levels. The owner of the information is responsible for classifying it; when in doubt, treat information as Confidential.

LevelDescriptionExamples
PublicApproved for public release.Marketing website, published blog posts, job postings.
InternalFor Lumora personnel only; low harm if disclosed.Org charts, internal announcements, most policies.
ConfidentialSignificant harm if disclosed.Source code, product roadmaps, customer lists, contracts, non-public financials.
RestrictedSevere harm if disclosed; legal or contractual protection.Customer production data, payroll and employee personal data, health information, encryption keys, credentials.

Each employee's PeopleHub profile records the highest data access level granted to them ("Internal", "Confidential", or "Restricted"). Restricted access is granted only on a need-to-know basis and is reviewed quarterly.

3. Account Security

  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all Lumora accounts. Hardware security keys are required for anyone with Restricted access.
  • Passwords must be at least 14 characters, unique to each system, and stored only in the Lumora-approved password manager.
  • Never share credentials, MFA codes, or session tokens with anyone, including IT staff. Lumora IT will never ask for your password.
  • Single sign-on (SSO) must be used for all applications that support it.

4. Devices

  • Only Lumora-managed devices (enrolled in mobile device management, with disk encryption and endpoint protection) may be used to access Confidential or Restricted information or Lumora internal systems.
  • Personal phones may be used for Lumora email, chat, and MFA only if enrolled in the Lumora mobile app management profile. Personal computers may not be used for Lumora work.
  • Devices must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity and must be locked manually whenever unattended.
  • Operating system and security updates must be installed within 7 days of release, or within 48 hours for updates marked critical.
  • Removable storage (USB drives, external hard drives) is blocked by default; exceptions require Information Security approval.
  • Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT and Information Security within 24 hours, and as soon as possible if the device contained Restricted information (see also POL-011).

5. Network and Remote Access

  • Use the Lumora VPN whenever accessing internal systems from any network other than a Lumora office network, including home networks.
  • Public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports, hotels, co-working spaces) may be used only with the VPN connected.
  • Home routers should use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption with a non-default administrator password.
  • Work involving Confidential or Restricted information must not be performed where screens or conversations can be observed by others, such as on public transport or in cafes. Privacy screens are available from IT.

6. Data Handling

  • Store Company information only in approved systems (Lumora Drive, the code repository, approved SaaS applications). Do not store Lumora data in personal email or personal cloud storage.
  • Do not forward Lumora email to personal accounts.
  • Share Confidential information externally only under a signed non-disclosure agreement and through approved sharing tools. Restricted information may be shared externally only with Information Security and Legal approval.
  • Print Confidential or Restricted information only when necessary, collect printouts immediately, and shred them when no longer needed. Printing on shared or public printers (hotels, co-working spaces) is prohibited.
  • Employee personal data and health information may be accessed only by authorized People Team members and only for legitimate purposes.

7. Use of AI Tools

Employees may use Lumora-approved AI tools listed on the Security intranet page. The following rules apply:

  • Public or Internal information may be used with any approved AI tool.
  • Confidential information may be used only with AI tools that are approved for Confidential data and covered by a Lumora enterprise agreement that prohibits training on our data.
  • Restricted information, including customer production data and employee personal data, must never be entered into any AI tool unless the tool has been specifically approved by Information Security for that data set.
  • Outputs of AI tools must be reviewed by a human for accuracy before being used in decisions affecting customers or employees.

The Lumora HR assistant is an approved internal tool. It uses only synthetic or employee-provided information, retrieves official policies, and does not make employment decisions.

8. International Travel and Work From Abroad

Employees traveling internationally, or working temporarily from abroad under POL-005, must:

  • Obtain Information Security approval as part of the Work From Abroad request (POL-005 section 4.3).
  • Use a travel loaner laptop when traveling to any country designated high-risk by Information Security, and when required for employees with Restricted access.
  • Keep devices on their person or locked in a hotel safe; never check devices in luggage.
  • Expect that access to certain systems may be geographically restricted while abroad.
  • Report any device inspection or seizure by border officials to Information Security within 24 hours.

9. Acceptable Use

Lumora systems may not be used to:

  • Access, store, or transmit illegal, harassing, discriminatory, or sexually explicit material.
  • Install unapproved software, browser extensions, or remote-access tools.
  • Circumvent security controls, including disabling endpoint protection or the VPN.
  • Mine cryptocurrency or operate personal commercial ventures.
  • Scan networks or test security controls without written authorization from Information Security.

Limited personal use (for example, checking personal news or making personal appointments) is acceptable if it is brief and does not violate this policy.

10. Security Incident Reporting

A security incident is any event that may compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of Lumora systems or data, including phishing emails that were clicked, malware alerts, lost devices, misdirected emails containing Confidential or Restricted data, and suspected unauthorized access. Report suspected incidents to Information Security immediately, and in any case within 1 hour of discovery, via security@lumora.example or the #security-help channel. Do not attempt to investigate on your own, and do not delete evidence.

11. Monitoring

To the extent permitted by local law, Lumora monitors its systems, networks, and devices for security purposes. Monitoring is conducted by authorized personnel and is subject to privacy laws in each jurisdiction, including the UK GDPR for London-based employees.

12. Training

All personnel must complete Security Awareness training within 7 days of their start date (see POL-010) and annually thereafter, and must complete periodic phishing simulations. Employees with Restricted access complete an additional annual module.

13. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in loss of system access and disciplinary action up to and including termination, and may be reported to law enforcement where required.
