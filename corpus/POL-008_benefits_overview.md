# Benefits Overview

Document ID: POL-008
Owner: People Team (Total Rewards)
Effective date: January 1, 2026 (plan year January 1 to December 31, 2026)
Applies to: Employees of Lumora Systems, Inc. in the United States. Canadian and UK benefits are summarized in section 13.

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was created for an educational AI engineering project. Plan names and amounts are illustrative. The official plan documents govern in case of any conflict.

## 1. Purpose

This overview describes the health, financial, and wellbeing benefits available to Lumora employees in the United States, who is eligible, when coverage begins, and how to enroll or make changes. Employees should consult the official plan documents and summaries of benefits and coverage in the Benefits portal (BenefitsHub) for complete details.

Eligible dependents include a legal spouse or registered domestic partner, children up to age 26 (regardless of student or marital status), and children of any age who are incapable of self-support due to a disability.

## 3. When Coverage Begins

Coverage for medical, dental, vision, life, and disability begins on the **first day of the month following the employee's start date**, or on the start date itself if it falls on the first day of a month. New employees must enroll within **30 days** of their start date. Employees who do not enroll within 30 days receive default coverage (employee-only Lumora PPO medical, dental, and vision, plus basic life and disability) and may not change elections until the next open enrollment unless they have a qualifying life event.

## 4. Medical Plans

Lumora offers two medical plans administered by a national carrier:

### 4.1 Lumora PPO

- In-network deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family.
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family.
- Primary care visit copay: $25; specialist copay: $50.
- Employee premium contribution: Lumora pays 90% of the premium for employees and 75% for dependents.

### 4.2 Lumora HDHP with HSA

- In-network deductible: $1,700 individual / $3,400 family.
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $4,000 individual / $8,000 family.
- Preventive care is covered at 100% before the deductible.
- Employee premium contribution: Lumora pays 100% of the premium for employees and 85% for dependents.
- Lumora contributes to the employee's Health Savings Account (HSA): **$750 per year for employee-only coverage and $1,500 per year for family coverage**, deposited in equal amounts each pay period. Employees may contribute additional pre-tax amounts up to the IRS annual limit.

Employees may not be enrolled in both an HSA and a general-purpose Flexible Spending Account (FSA).

## 5. Dental and Vision

- **Dental:** preventive care covered at 100%, basic services at 80%, major services at 50%, and orthodontia (children and adults) at 50% up to a $2,000 lifetime maximum. Annual maximum benefit: $2,000 per person.
- **Vision:** annual eye exam with a $10 copay; $200 allowance for frames or contact lenses every 12 months.
- Lumora pays 100% of dental and vision premiums for full-time employees and 50% for eligible part-time employees.

## 6. Flexible Spending Accounts

- **Health Care FSA:** contribute pre-tax up to the IRS limit for eligible medical, dental, and vision expenses. Up to $640 may be carried over to the next plan year.
- **Limited Purpose FSA:** for employees enrolled in the HDHP; covers dental and vision expenses only.
- **Dependent Care FSA:** contribute up to $5,000 per household per year pre-tax for eligible childcare and elder-care expenses.

## 7. Retirement: 401(k) Plan

- Eligible employees may contribute from the first paycheck, pre-tax or Roth, up to the IRS annual limit.
- Employees are automatically enrolled at 4% of eligible pay unless they choose a different rate or opt out within 30 days.
- **Company match:** Lumora matches 100% of the first 4% of eligible pay contributed, plus 50% of the next 2%, for a maximum match of 5% of eligible pay.
- **Vesting:** employee contributions are always 100% vested. Company matching contributions vest one-third per year of service, and are fully vested after 3 years of service.

## 8. Life and Disability Insurance

- **Basic life and AD&D:** 2 times annual base salary, up to a maximum of $500,000, provided at no cost.
- **Supplemental life:** employees may purchase additional coverage for themselves, their spouse or partner, and children.
- **Short-term disability (STD):** pays 60% of base salary for up to 12 weeks after a 7-day waiting period. Lumora tops up STD to 100% of base salary for the first 6 weeks (see the Leave of Absence Policy, POL-009).
- **Long-term disability (LTD):** pays 60% of base salary, up to $15,000 per month, after 90 days of disability.

## 9. Wellbeing Benefits

- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** free, confidential counseling (up to 8 sessions per issue per year), legal and financial consultations, and referrals, available 24/7 to employees and household members.
- **Wellness stipend:** $50 per month (taxable), which may be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, meditation apps, or similar.
- **Mental health:** therapy and psychiatry are covered under both medical plans at in-network rates.

## 10. Learning and Development Stipend

Full-time employees receive a learning stipend of **$1,500 per calendar year** for courses, certifications, conferences, and professional books related to their current role or a reasonable career path at Lumora. Unused amounts do not carry over. Requests over $500 require manager pre-approval in ExpenseIQ (POL-006 section 3). If an employee voluntarily leaves within 6 months of a reimbursement over $1,000, the employee may be asked to repay it where permitted by law.

## 11. Other Benefits

- **Paid parental leave, bereavement leave, and other leaves:** see POL-009.
- **Holidays and PTO:** see POL-002 and POL-003.
- **Home office stipend:** see POL-011.

## 12. Open Enrollment and Qualifying Life Events

### 12.1 Open enrollment

Open enrollment for the 2027 plan year runs from **November 2, 2026 through November 20, 2026**. During open enrollment employees may change medical, dental, and vision plans, add or remove dependents, and enroll in or change FSA elections. FSA elections must be made every year; other elections roll over if no change is made. Changes take effect January 1, 2027.

### 12.2 Qualifying life events

Outside open enrollment, employees may change elections only within **30 days** of a qualifying life event, such as marriage or registration of a domestic partnership, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, death of a dependent, a spouse's change in employment that affects coverage, or a move outside the plan's service area. Documentation (such as a marriage or birth certificate) must be submitted in BenefitsHub within the same 30 days. Changes are effective from the date of the event for births and adoptions, and from the first day of the following month for other events.

## 13. Canada and United Kingdom

- **Canada (Ontario):** extended health and dental through a group plan provider, with coverage beginning on the start date; a Group RRSP with a Lumora match of up to 4% of base salary; life and disability insurance; and the EAP.
- **United Kingdom:** private medical insurance for employee and family, a workplace pension with a Lumora contribution of 6% of qualifying earnings (employee minimum 3%), life assurance of 4 times salary, income protection, and the EAP.

Learning and wellness stipends apply in Canada and the UK in local currency equivalents.

## 14. Changes Due to Employment Type

When an employee changes from full-time to part-time (or vice versa), benefits eligibility changes on the first day of the month following the change. An employee moving from full-time to part-time below 30 hours per week loses medical eligibility and will receive COBRA continuation information.

## 15. Leaving Lumora

Medical, dental, and vision coverage ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends. US employees are offered COBRA continuation coverage. HSA balances belong to the employee. Vested 401(k) balances remain the employee's property.

## 16. Using the HR Assistant for Benefits Questions

The HR assistant can look up an employee's current elections and eligibility from BenefitsHub data and explain plan features with citations to this overview. The assistant does not give personal medical, tax, or financial advice, and complex eligibility questions are escalated to the Benefits team through a PeopleHub case.
