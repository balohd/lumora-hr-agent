# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Document ID: POL-004
Owner: People Team (Workplace Programs)
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: All regular employees of Lumora Systems, Inc. Contractors follow the terms of their contract.

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was created for an educational AI engineering project.

## 1. Purpose

Lumora supports flexible work arrangements that let employees do their best work while maintaining collaboration, security, and legal compliance. This policy defines Lumora's work arrangements, who is eligible for each, how to request a change, and the expectations that apply when working away from a Lumora office. Working temporarily from a location other than an employee's approved work location (for example, another US state or another country) is governed by the International and Cross-Border Work Policy (POL-005), which must be read together with this policy.

## 2. Work Arrangements

Every employee has one of three approved work arrangements recorded in PeopleHub:

- **Onsite:** the employee works from an assigned Lumora office (Austin, Denver, Toronto, or London) five days per week, except for occasional flexibility approved by their manager.
- **Hybrid:** the employee works from an assigned Lumora office on at least 2 "anchor days" per week (Tuesday and Wednesday for most teams, or as set by the department head) and may work from their home address on other days.
- **Remote:** the employee works primarily from an approved home address in a country and state/province where Lumora is registered as an employer, and travels to an office for team events as requested (typically up to 4 times per year).

An employee's "approved work location" is the office or home address recorded in PeopleHub. Payroll taxes, benefits, and employment law obligations are based on this approved work location.

## 3. Eligibility

### 3.1 Hybrid eligibility

All employees in roles designated "hybrid-eligible" or "remote-eligible" may work a hybrid arrangement after completing their first 30 days of employment, subject to manager approval.

### 3.2 Remote eligibility

To be approved for a fully remote arrangement, all of the following must be true:

1. The employee's role is designated "remote-eligible" in the Lumora job architecture. Roles that require physical presence (for example, Facilities, IT Field Support, and Office Operations) are not remote-eligible. Customer Support roles are hybrid-eligible but not remote-eligible.
2. The employee has completed the 90-day introductory period.
3. The employee's most recent performance rating is "Meets Expectations" or higher, and the employee is not on a performance improvement plan.
4. The proposed home address is in a US state, Canadian province, or the United Kingdom where Lumora is registered as an employer. As of 2026 Lumora is registered in Texas, Colorado, California, New York, Washington, Illinois, Massachusetts, Ontario, and England.
5. The employee's manager and department head approve the request.

Interns are not eligible for remote arrangements. Contractors' location requirements are set by their contracts.

## 4. Requesting a Work Arrangement Change

Employees request a change of work arrangement through the "Work Arrangement Change" form in PeopleHub. The request routes to the manager, then the department head, then the People Team for a compliance review (registration, payroll, and benefits). The People Team aims to complete its review within 10 business days. A permanent move to a new state or country is a change of approved work location and must be approved before the move; moving without approval may result in loss of the remote arrangement or other action.

## 5. Temporary Work From Another Location

Occasionally working from a location other than the approved work location (for example, working from a family member's home in another state, or extending a vacation abroad with some working days) is called "temporary remote work" and is governed by POL-005. In summary:

- Working temporarily from another location within the same state or province requires only manager awareness.
- Working temporarily from another US state is allowed for up to 20 workdays (4 weeks) per calendar year with manager approval; longer periods require People Team and Payroll Tax review.
- Working temporarily from another country is allowed for up to 20 workdays in any rolling 12-month period, from approved countries only, with manager, People Team, and Information Security approval, requested at least 30 calendar days in advance.

Periods longer than these limits, such as six consecutive weeks abroad, exceed the standard allowance and require a Global Mobility exception (POL-005 section 7). Exceptions are rarely granted and are never guaranteed.

## 6. Expectations When Working Remotely

### 6.1 Availability and collaboration

Remote and hybrid employees are expected to be available during their team's core collaboration hours (10:00 to 15:00 in the team's primary time zone, unless the department head sets different hours), respond to messages within a reasonable time, attend scheduled meetings with video enabled when practical, and keep their calendar and status up to date.

### 6.2 Workspace

Employees must have a safe, quiet, and private workspace suitable for confidential conversations, and a reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps download. Remote and hybrid employees may be eligible for a home office stipend and a monthly internet reimbursement as described in the Equipment and Home Office Policy (POL-011) and the Travel and Expense Policy (POL-006).

### 6.3 Security

Remote work must comply with the Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy (POL-007), including:

- Using only Lumora-managed devices for Lumora work.
- Connecting through the Lumora VPN when accessing internal systems from any network other than a Lumora office.
- Never working on Confidential or Restricted information in public spaces where screens or conversations can be observed.
- Locking devices whenever they are unattended, and never leaving devices in vehicles.

### 6.4 Health and safety

Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe home workspace. Work-related injuries that occur in the home workspace during working hours must be reported to the People Team within 24 hours. Ergonomic assessments are available on request (POL-011).

## 7. Office Attendance for Remote Employees

Remote employees may be asked to travel to a Lumora office or other location for onboarding, team planning, and Company events. Travel for these purposes is reimbursed under POL-006. Remote employees who live within 50 miles (80 km) of a Lumora office may be asked to attend the office for occasional team days.

## 8. Changes and Revocation

Work arrangements are not guaranteed and may be changed by Lumora based on business needs, role changes, performance concerns, or policy non-compliance. Lumora will give at least 30 days' notice before requiring a remote employee to begin working onsite, except in cases of serious misconduct or security violations.

## 9. Relocation

A permanent relocation that changes an employee's approved work location (for example, moving from Texas to Colorado, or from the US to Canada) requires prior approval through the Work Arrangement Change form. Compensation may be adjusted to the pay zone of the new location. International relocations require a transfer to the local Lumora entity and are handled by Global Mobility; they are not available in countries where Lumora does not have an entity.

## 10. Using the HR Assistant for Remote Work Questions

The HR assistant can review an employee's work arrangement and eligibility factors, check a temporary remote work request against POL-004 and POL-005 limits, and draft a request message. The assistant's output is guidance only; approvals happen in PeopleHub.
