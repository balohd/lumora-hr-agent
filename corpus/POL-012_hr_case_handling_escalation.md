# HR Case Handling and Escalation Procedure

Document ID: POL-012
Owner: People Team (Employee Relations and People Operations)
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: People Team members, managers, and the Lumora HR assistant. Employees may read this procedure to understand how their requests are handled.

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was created for an educational AI engineering project.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure explains how HR requests and concerns ("cases") are categorized, prioritized, routed, and escalated, the response times employees can expect, and how confidentiality is protected. It also sets out what the Lumora HR assistant may and may not do.

## 2. Case Channels

Employees may open a case in PeopleHub, email people@lumora.example, contact Employee Relations at er@lumora.example, or call the Lumora Ethics Line (POL-001 section 12). Managers who receive a concern that falls under Severity 1 must open a case in PeopleHub within 1 business day; they must not investigate it themselves.

## 3. Case Categories

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| General inquiry | Policy questions, holiday dates, how to use PeopleHub |
| Time off and leave | PTO questions, leave of absence requests, bereavement |
| Benefits | Enrollment, eligibility, qualifying life events |
| Payroll | Missing or incorrect pay, tax forms, expense reimbursement delays |
| Work arrangement | Remote or hybrid requests, work from abroad, relocation |
| Accommodation | Disability, pregnancy, or religious accommodation |
| Employee relations | Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, bullying, conflicts with manager, misconduct |
| Safety | Threats, violence, unsafe conditions, self-harm concerns |

## 4. Severity Levels and Response Times

| Severity | Description | Examples | Initial response | Routed to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 (Critical) | Potential legal exposure, safety risk, or allegations of serious misconduct | Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, threats or violence, self-harm concerns, whistleblower reports | Within 4 business hours; Employee Relations engaged within 24 hours | Employee Relations (and Legal or Security as needed) |
| Severity 2 (High) | Time-sensitive matters affecting pay, health, or legal rights | Accommodation requests, leave of absence, payroll errors, benefits enrollment problems, work-from-abroad exceptions | Within 1 business day; resolution target 3 business days | Relevant specialist team |
| Severity 3 (Standard) | General requests | Policy questions, PTO guidance, document requests | Within 2 business days; resolution target 5 business days | People Operations |

If anyone is in immediate danger, call emergency services first (POL-001 section 7).

## 5. Confidentiality

Case information is shared only with people who need it to resolve the case. Medical information is held by CareBridge Leave Services (POL-009). Employee relations cases are visible only to Employee Relations and, where needed, Legal. Employees will be told who will have access to their information. Lumora cannot promise complete confidentiality where disclosure is necessary to investigate, prevent harm, or comply with law.

## 6. The Lumora HR Assistant

### 6.1 What the assistant may do

The HR assistant is an AI tool that helps employees with HR questions. It may:

- Answer questions using official Lumora policies, citing the specific document and section.
- Look up an employee's own information (profile, PTO balance, benefits status) for the purpose of answering their question.
- Check a proposed request (for example, PTO dates, a work-from-abroad request, or an expense) against policy rules and explain which approvals are required.
- Draft messages, such as a PTO request to a manager or a case summary, for the employee to review and send themselves.
- Create a mock or draft HR case only after the employee explicitly confirms that they want it created.

### 6.2 What the assistant must not do

The assistant must not:

- Make, approve, or deny employment decisions, including PTO approvals, leave approvals, accommodations, or disciplinary actions.
- Investigate, assess the merits of, or attempt to resolve Severity 1 matters such as harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or threats. For these, it must provide the reporting channels in POL-001 section 12, recommend escalation to Employee Relations, and offer to prepare a confidential case summary for the employee to submit.
- Provide legal, medical, tax, or immigration advice.
- Answer questions that are not covered by Lumora policy as if they were policy. When policy evidence is missing or incomplete, it must say so and suggest contacting the People Team.
- Submit, send, or create anything on the employee's behalf without explicit confirmation.
- Disclose another employee's personal information.

### 6.3 Distinguishing policy from recommendations

When responding, the assistant must clearly distinguish between statements of policy (with citations) and suggestions or recommended next steps (which are not policy).

## 7. Escalation Rules

A case must be escalated to the next level when:

- It is Severity 1 (always escalated to Employee Relations).
- An employee requests an exception to policy (for example, a Global Mobility exception under POL-005 section 7, or PTO during a blackout period).
- The case is not resolved within its resolution target.
- The employee disagrees with the outcome and asks for a review; reviews are conducted by a People Team member who was not involved in the original decision.
- The matter involves a senior leader (Director or above) as a subject of a complaint; such cases are routed to the VP of People and Legal.

## 8. Case Records

Every case record contains: case ID, category, severity, summary, the employee's ID, related policy references, status, owner, and timestamps. Case records are retained for 7 years after closure, or longer where required by law or litigation hold.

## 9. Closing a Case

A case is closed when the employee confirms the issue is resolved or when the People Team has provided a final response and 5 business days have passed without a reply. For Severity 1 cases, the employee is informed when the investigation is concluded, though details of any disciplinary action taken against another person may not be shared.
