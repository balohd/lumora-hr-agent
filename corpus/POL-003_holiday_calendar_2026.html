Holiday Calendar and Floating Holiday Policy 2026

Document ID: POL-003
Owner: People Team (Total Rewards)
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: All regular employees of Lumora Systems, Inc.

Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was created for an educational AI engineering project.

1. Purpose

This policy lists the paid Company holidays observed in each Lumora location for calendar year 2026 and explains how floating holidays, holiday pay, and work on holidays are handled. Company holidays are separate from Paid Time Off (PTO, see POL-002) and are not deducted from an employee's PTO balance.

2. Eligibility

Regular full-time employees receive holiday pay for each Company holiday observed in their work location. Regular part-time employees receive holiday pay prorated by their FTE percentage when the holiday falls on a regularly scheduled workday. Interns receive holiday pay for holidays that fall on a scheduled workday. Independent contractors do not receive holiday pay. Employees on an unpaid leave of absence do not receive holiday pay for holidays that fall during the leave.

6. Floating Holidays

US and Canadian employees receive 2 floating holidays per calendar year. Floating holidays may be used for any reason, including cultural or religious observances, personal celebrations, or a day of rest. Floating holidays:

  • Are credited on January 1 (employees hired after June 30 receive 1 floating holiday in their first year).
  • Must be requested in PeopleHub with at least 5 business days' notice and approved by the manager, using the same process as PTO.
  • Do not carry over to the next year and are not paid out at termination unless required by law.
  • Must be taken as a full day.

7. Holidays Falling on a Weekend

When a fixed holiday falls on a Saturday, it is generally observed on the preceding Friday; when it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday. In the UK and Canada, substitute days follow the statutory rules of the jurisdiction. The observed dates in sections 3 to 5 already reflect these rules for 2026.

8. Working on a Holiday

Employees who are required by their manager to work on a Company holiday (for example, on-call engineers or Customer Support staff on a holiday rotation) receive a substitute day off to be taken within 60 days. Non-exempt (hourly) US employees who work on a Company holiday are paid at 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked, in addition to the substitute day. Requirements under local law in Canada and the UK (for example, Ontario public holiday pay) apply where more generous.

9. Remote Employees and Employees Working From Another Location

Employees observe the holiday calendar of the country of their employing entity, not the country where they are temporarily working. For example, a US employee approved to work temporarily from Portugal under the International and Cross-Border Work Policy (POL-005) observes US Company holidays, not Portuguese public holidays.

10. Holidays During Leave or PTO

A Company holiday that falls during approved PTO is paid as a holiday and not deducted from PTO. A holiday that falls during a paid leave of absence (for example, paid parental leave under POL-009) does not extend the leave.
