# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Document ID: POL-002
Owner: People Team (Total Rewards)
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: Regular full-time and part-time employees of Lumora Systems, Inc. in the United States. Employees in Canada and the United Kingdom follow this policy except where local law provides a greater entitlement (see section 12).

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was created for an educational AI engineering project.

## 1. Purpose

Lumora believes that rest is essential to doing great work. Paid Time Off (PTO) gives employees flexible paid time away from work for vacation, personal business, religious observances, or any other reason. PTO is separate from paid sick time (section 9), Company holidays and floating holidays (see the Holiday Calendar, POL-003), and leaves of absence (see the Leave of Absence Policy, POL-009).

## 2. Eligibility

- Regular full-time employees (scheduled 30 or more hours per week) are eligible for PTO from their first day of employment.
- Regular part-time employees (scheduled 20 to 29 hours per week) accrue PTO on a prorated basis according to their FTE (full-time equivalent) percentage. For example, an employee at 0.6 FTE accrues 60% of the full-time rate.
- Interns accrue PTO at a flat rate of 0.5 days per month worked and may not carry over unused PTO.
- Independent contractors and agency temporary workers are not Lumora employees and are not eligible for PTO. Contractors should arrange time away with their agency or according to their contract.

## 3. Accrual Rates

PTO accrues each pay period based on length of continuous service as of the start of the pay period. Full-time accrual rates are:

| Years of service | Annual PTO (days) | Monthly accrual (days) |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 2 years | 15 | 1.25 |
| 2 years to less than 5 years | 20 | 1.67 |
| 5 years or more | 25 | 2.08 |

A "day" equals the employee's normally scheduled hours for one workday (8 hours for a standard full-time schedule). PTO is recorded in PeopleHub in days to two decimal places.

## 4. Maximum Balance (Accrual Cap)

PTO balances may not exceed 30 days for full-time employees (prorated for part-time employees). Once a balance reaches the cap, accrual pauses until the employee uses PTO and the balance drops below the cap. Lost accrual during a pause cannot be recovered. PeopleHub sends an automated reminder when a balance reaches 25 days.

## 5. Carryover

Up to 5 days of unused PTO may be carried over from one calendar year into the next. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year; any carried-over days still unused on March 31 are forfeited. Days above the 5-day carryover limit are forfeited on December 31 unless local law requires otherwise. Interns may not carry over PTO.

## 6. Requesting PTO

### 6.1 How to request

All PTO must be requested in PeopleHub and approved by the employee's direct manager before the time is taken. Verbal or chat approvals are not sufficient on their own; the manager must approve the request in PeopleHub so that payroll records are accurate. The employee should also add the time to their shared calendar and set an out-of-office message.

### 6.2 Advance notice requirements

Employees must submit PTO requests with the following minimum notice:

| Length of PTO request | Minimum advance notice |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 consecutive workdays | 3 business days |
| 3 to 5 consecutive workdays | 10 business days (2 weeks) |
| More than 5 consecutive workdays | 20 business days (4 weeks) |

Managers may approve requests submitted with less notice at their discretion, for example when workload allows, and should not unreasonably deny short-notice requests for personal emergencies. A request submitted with less than the required notice is a "short-notice request" and must include a brief note explaining the circumstances.

### 6.3 Approval criteria

Managers consider team coverage, business needs, and fairness when approving PTO. Requests are generally approved on a first-come, first-served basis when multiple team members request overlapping time. Managers should respond to PTO requests within 3 business days. If a request is denied, the manager must provide a reason and work with the employee to identify alternative dates.

### 6.4 Extended PTO

Requests for more than 10 consecutive workdays of PTO require approval from both the direct manager and the department head (second-level manager), and the People Team must be notified through PeopleHub. Combining PTO with a leave of absence is governed by POL-009.

## 7. Blackout Periods

Certain teams have periods during which PTO is restricted because of critical business cycles:

- Finance and Accounting: the last 5 business days of each fiscal quarter and the first 5 business days of January (year-end close). PTO during these periods requires approval from the Controller.
- Customer Support: December 15 through January 5. PTO during this period requires approval from the Director of Customer Support, and requests are limited to ensure at least 70% of the team is available each day.
- Engineering on-call: employees scheduled for primary on-call rotation must arrange a swap before requesting PTO during their on-call week.

Blackout periods do not apply to paid sick time, bereavement leave, or protected leaves of absence.

## 8. Negative Balances

With manager approval, an employee may take PTO before it has been accrued, up to a negative balance of 3 days (prorated for part-time employees). Negative balances are not available to interns or to employees in their first 90 days. If employment ends while the balance is negative, the negative amount will be deducted from the final paycheck where permitted by law.

## 9. Paid Sick Time

Paid sick time is separate from PTO. Full-time employees receive 7 days of paid sick time per calendar year, credited on January 1 (prorated for employees hired mid-year and for part-time employees). Where state or local law provides a greater sick-time entitlement (for example, Colorado's Healthy Families and Workplaces Act), the greater entitlement applies.

- Sick time may be used for the employee's own illness, injury, or medical appointments, or to care for a family member.
- Advance notice is not required for unforeseeable illness; employees should notify their manager as early as practicable, ideally before the start of the workday.
- A doctor's note may be requested only after 3 or more consecutive days of sick time, unless local law provides otherwise.
- Unused sick time does not carry over (unless required by law) and is not paid out at termination.
- Absences beyond the available sick time may qualify for a leave of absence or short-term disability under POL-009.

## 10. Holidays During PTO

If a Company holiday (POL-003) falls during an employee's approved PTO, the holiday is not deducted from the PTO balance.

## 11. PTO at Termination

Unused, accrued PTO is paid out at termination where required by applicable state law (for example, in Colorado and California). In states that do not require payout, such as Texas, accrued unused PTO is not paid out unless the employee provides at least two weeks' notice of resignation and works through the notice period, in which case up to 10 days of accrued PTO will be paid. Carried-over days and floating holidays are treated the same way as other PTO for payout purposes only where the law requires it.

## 12. International Employees

Employees in Canada and the United Kingdom receive at least the statutory minimum annual leave in their province or country, plus any Lumora enhancement:

- Canada (Ontario): accrual follows the US schedule in section 3, which exceeds the Ontario statutory minimum. Vacation pay requirements under the Employment Standards Act apply.
- United Kingdom: employees receive 25 days of annual leave plus UK bank holidays (POL-003), rising to 27 days after 5 years of service. UK carryover is limited to 5 days, to be used by March 31.

## 13. Using the HR Assistant for PTO Questions

The HR assistant can check an employee's current PTO balance, explain notice and approval requirements, and draft a PTO request message to the employee's manager. The assistant cannot submit or approve PTO in PeopleHub; the employee must submit the request and the manager must approve it there.

## 14. Questions

Questions about PTO should be directed to the People Team at people@lumora.example or through a PeopleHub case.
