# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document ID: POL-006
Owner: Finance (Accounts Payable), with the People Team
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: All employees and interns of Lumora Systems, Inc. Contractors are reimbursed only where their contract provides for it.

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was created for an educational AI engineering project.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Lumora reimburses, the limits that apply, and how to submit expenses. Employees should spend Company money as carefully as they would their own. Amounts are in US dollars; employees in Canada and the UK should apply the equivalent amounts in local currency listed in ExpenseIQ.

## 2. General Principles

An expense is reimbursable only if it is:

1. Incurred for a legitimate Lumora business purpose;
2. Reasonable in amount and consistent with the limits in this policy;
3. Pre-approved where this policy requires it (section 3); and
4. Submitted in ExpenseIQ with an itemized receipt within the required timeframe (section 4).

Expenses that are primarily for personal benefit are not reimbursable, even if incurred during business travel.

## 3. Pre-Approval Requirements

The following require approval from the employee's manager in ExpenseIQ before the expense is incurred:

- Any single expense greater than $500.
- All international business travel.
- Conference and training registrations (also subject to the learning stipend in the Benefits Overview, POL-008).
- Client entertainment of any amount.
- Any purchase of hardware, software, or subscriptions (these also require IT approval; see section 9).

Expenses greater than $5,000 additionally require department head approval.

## 4. Submitting Expenses

- Expenses must be submitted in ExpenseIQ within 30 days of the date incurred (or within 30 days of returning from a trip).
- Itemized receipts are required for every expense of $25 or more. For expenses below $25, a description of the business purpose is sufficient.
- Each report must include the business purpose, the attendees for any meal, and the project or cost center.
- Expenses submitted more than 90 days after being incurred will not be reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances approved by the Controller.
- Approved expenses are reimbursed through payroll direct deposit, typically within 10 business days of final approval.

## 5. Air Travel

- Book through Lumora's travel booking tool, TripDesk, whenever possible.
- Economy class is standard for all flights.
- Premium economy is permitted for flights with a scheduled flight time of more than 6 hours.
- Business class is permitted only for flights with a scheduled flight time of more than 8 hours, with prior approval from a Vice President.
- Seat upgrades, priority boarding, and lounge access are personal expenses unless covered by the above.
- One checked bag is reimbursable per flight; additional bags require a business reason (for example, carrying demo equipment).

## 6. Lodging

- Standard nightly limit (excluding taxes): $250 per night.
- High-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, Boston, Seattle, Washington DC, London, Toronto, Zurich, and Tokyo): $350 per night.
- Stays above these limits require manager approval and a written explanation (for example, a conference hotel).
- Short-term rentals are permitted if the total cost is at or below the equivalent hotel cost.
- Room service, minibar, movies, and laundry for trips shorter than 5 nights are not reimbursable.

## 7. Meals and Per Diem

- During business travel, meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to a daily maximum of $75 for domestic travel and $100 for international travel, including tax and tip.
- Tips should not exceed 20%.
- Meals during the workday when not traveling are not reimbursable, except for approved team events or client meetings.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable, except as part of approved client entertainment (section 8).

## 8. Client Entertainment

Client entertainment must be pre-approved by the employee's manager and must have a clear business purpose. The limit is $150 per person, including tax and tip. Alcohol in moderation is reimbursable only as part of approved client entertainment. Gifts to clients are limited to $100 per recipient per year and must comply with the Code of Conduct (POL-001 section 9). Entertainment of government officials requires prior Legal approval.

## 9. Technology, Equipment, and Home Office Expenses

- **Laptops, monitors, and mobile devices** are provided by IT under the Equipment and Home Office Policy (POL-011). Personal purchases of laptops, tablets, or phones are **not reimbursable**, even if used for work, because only Lumora-managed devices may be used for Lumora work (POL-007).
- **Software and subscriptions** must be approved by IT before purchase; unapproved software purchases are not reimbursable.
- **Home office furniture**, such as an ergonomic chair or desk, is not reimbursed as an individual expense. Instead, eligible remote and hybrid employees receive a home office stipend under POL-011 section 5, which is claimed in ExpenseIQ under the "Home Office Stipend" category and counts against the stipend limit.
- **Internet:** remote employees may claim up to $50 per month and hybrid employees up to $25 per month for home internet service, with a monthly bill as the receipt. Onsite employees are not eligible.
- **Mobile phone:** employees in roles with an on-call requirement receive a $40 monthly mobile phone stipend through payroll; other employees are not reimbursed for mobile phone costs.

## 10. Ground Transportation and Mileage

- Taxis, rideshares, and public transit are reimbursable for business travel.
- Rental cars should be compact or mid-size; insurance purchased from the rental company is reimbursable for international rentals only.
- Use of a personal vehicle for business purposes is reimbursed at the IRS standard mileage rate in effect on the date of travel (or the CRA or HMRC approved rate for Canadian and UK employees), as published in ExpenseIQ.
- Normal commuting between home and the employee's approved work location is not reimbursable.
- Parking and tolls incurred on business trips are reimbursable; parking fines and traffic tickets are not.

## 11. Travel Safety and Security

Employees traveling internationally on business must register their trip in TripDesk, which provides travel risk alerts. Travel to countries designated high-risk by Information Security requires the use of a travel loaner laptop (POL-007). Business travel is distinct from temporary remote work; costs of temporary remote work chosen for personal reasons are not reimbursable (POL-005 section 9).

## 12. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Non-reimbursable expenses include, but are not limited to: personal entertainment; in-flight wi-fi for personal use; fines and penalties; personal travel insurance; childcare and pet care; gym fees (see the wellness stipend in POL-008); clothing and personal grooming; expenses for spouses or guests; personal laptops, tablets, or phones; furniture outside the home office stipend; and expenses already reimbursed by a third party.

## 13. Corporate Card

Employees who are expected to incur more than $2,000 per year in business expenses may request a Lumora corporate card. Corporate card charges must be reconciled in ExpenseIQ within 30 days. Personal use of the corporate card is prohibited.

## 14. Audits and Misuse

Finance audits expense reports regularly. Submitting false or inflated expenses, splitting a purchase to avoid an approval threshold, or seeking duplicate reimbursement is a violation of the Code of Conduct (POL-001) and may result in discipline up to and including termination.
