# Equipment and Home Office Policy

Document ID: POL-011
Owner: IT, with the People Team and Finance
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: All employees and interns of Lumora Systems, Inc. Contractors receive equipment only as specified in their statement of work.

Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was created for an educational AI engineering project.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains the equipment Lumora provides, the home office support available to remote and hybrid employees, employee responsibilities for Company equipment, and the process for repairs, replacement, loss, and return.

## 2. Standard Equipment

Every employee receives a Lumora-managed laptop configured by IT. The standard models are:

- Engineering, Data, and Design roles: MacBook Pro 14-inch with 32 GB memory.
- All other roles: MacBook Air 13-inch with 16 GB memory, or a Dell Latitude 14 for employees who require Windows.

Employees also receive a headset with a microphone, a laptop stand, an external keyboard, and a mouse. Office-based and hybrid employees have an external monitor at their assigned office desk. Remote employees receive one 27-inch external monitor shipped to their approved home address.

Employees with documented role requirements (for example, a second monitor for data analysts or a high-memory workstation for machine learning engineers) may request additional equipment through the IT Service Portal with manager approval.

## 3. Ownership and Management

All equipment provided by Lumora remains Company property. Laptops are enrolled in device management, encrypted, and protected with endpoint security software as required by the Information Security Policy (POL-007). Employees must not remove management profiles, disable security software, or allow anyone else (including family members) to use Company equipment.

Personal laptops and tablets may not be used for Lumora work, and Lumora does not reimburse personal purchases of laptops, tablets, or phones (POL-006 section 9).

## 4. Laptop Refresh

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years for Engineering, Data, and Design roles and every 4 years for all other roles, or earlier if the device fails and cannot be economically repaired. IT contacts employees when their device is eligible for refresh. The old device must be returned within 10 business days of receiving the replacement.

## 5. Home Office Stipend

### 5.1 Eligibility and amounts

To help employees set up a safe, ergonomic workspace at home, Lumora provides a one-time home office stipend:

- Remote employees: up to $500.
- Hybrid employees: up to $250.
- Onsite employees and interns: not eligible.

The stipend may be claimed once per 3-year period. Employees who change from hybrid to remote may claim the difference between the two amounts.

### 5.2 Eligible items

The stipend may be used for furniture and accessories for the home workspace, including:

- An ergonomic office chair.
- A desk or a sit-stand desk converter.
- Desk lighting.
- A footrest, monitor arm, or document holder.
- Cable management and surge protectors.

### 5.3 Ineligible items

The stipend may not be used for computers, tablets, phones, monitors (provided by IT), software, decorative items, general household furniture, or internet service (see the internet reimbursement in POL-006 section 9).

### 5.4 How to claim

Purchase eligible items and submit receipts in ExpenseIQ under the "Home Office Stipend" category within 30 days of purchase. New hires may claim the stipend at any time within their first 90 days and thereafter at any time in the 3-year period. Amounts above the stipend limit are the employee's responsibility. Items purchased with the stipend belong to the employee.

## 6. Ergonomics

Lumora offers virtual ergonomic assessments to all employees through the People Team. Employees with a medical need for specialized equipment (such as a specific chair or keyboard) beyond the stipend may request it as an accommodation through a PeopleHub case (POL-009 section 12). Approved accommodation equipment is purchased by Lumora and does not count against the stipend.

## 7. Repairs and Support

Report hardware problems through the IT Service Portal. IT will repair or replace defective equipment, generally shipping a replacement within 3 business days for remote employees. Employees must not attempt to repair Company equipment themselves or take it to third-party repair shops.

## 8. Loss, Theft, and Damage

- Report lost or stolen equipment to IT and Information Security within 24 hours, and immediately if the device may have contained Restricted information (POL-007 section 4). Theft should also be reported to local police, and the police report number provided to IT.
- Accidental damage is repaired or replaced at Lumora's cost. Repeated loss or damage resulting from negligence (for example, leaving a laptop unattended in a vehicle) may result in the employee being asked to contribute to replacement costs where permitted by law, and may be treated as a policy violation.

## 9. Return of Equipment

When employment ends, or when a device is replaced, employees must return all Company equipment (laptop, monitor, peripherals, badges, and security keys) within 10 business days. IT provides a prepaid return kit to remote employees. Furniture purchased with the home office stipend does not need to be returned. Where permitted by law, the replacement cost of unreturned equipment may be deducted from final pay or recovered by other means.

## 10. Travel Loaner Laptops

Travel loaner laptops are available from IT for travel to high-risk countries and for employees with Restricted access who are approved to work abroad (POL-005 section 6.3, POL-007 section 8). Request a loaner at least 10 business days before departure. Loaners are wiped on return.
